## 🚀 Why Choose Hunt Showdown Aimbot?

Hunt's time-to-kill is short and its weapons are unforgiving. The tool exposes the values that decide a shot: engagement window, travel speed, lead, sway, and stopping point. That makes a Sparks shot and a Winfield spray different setups instead of one compromise.

Per-weapon profiles keep those configurations independent, so changing a hunter loadout does not require retuning every slider.
